**Squiggle 2.0 is out.**

Squiggle now runs on Cloudflare, and the editor has a lot of new things to build with:

- Five new kits: World (tile levels with a camera and a tile painter), Scenes, Animate, Game Tools and 3D.
- Three game expansions for Together: Scores and Rounds, Teams and Items, and Arena. You can build Game Rules now. The game server that runs them is switched off in this release, and turning it on is what is next.
- A room panel, a presence bar, and a sync chip that shows whether your edits reached the room.
- Night and Day themes, a project checker, and the tile painter on phones.

Your account, your projects, your rooms and your play links all carry over. You do not need to sign up again.

If something looks wrong, post in #help or use /bug.
